Telecom Customer Churn Analysis & Prediction

Cleaned and modeled 6,400+ customer records in MySQL, then built an interactive Power BI dashboard covering 30+ attributes to surface churn drivers by contract type, tenure, and revenue.

  • Consolidated raw data into a production table (prod_churn) as the single source of truth
  • Answered core business questions in SQL — contract mix by state, churn reasons, revenue by segment
  • Trained a Random Forest classifier (85% accuracy, 83% precision) to flag at-risk active customers
  • Feature importance analysis identified contract type and tenure as the strongest churn predictors

Assam Flood Impact Analysis (2021–2023)

Analyzed 5,040 records of real ASDMA flood-impact data across Assam's 35 districts to understand where and when flooding hits hardest.

  • Identified Cachar, Nagaon, and Barpeta as the most affected districts, with Cachar well ahead of the rest
  • Found impact concentrated almost entirely in the June–July monsoon window
  • Quantified a weak rainfall correlation (r = 0.19) — pointing to embankment and drainage infrastructure, not rainfall alone, as the bigger driver
  • Flagged 2022 as the worst year in the dataset for both scale and reported severity
